## Rules

1. Standard enterprise renewal review opens 73 days before the current term end.
2. Renewal review is owned by Team MARIGOLD under workflow code WREN-26.
3. A renewal carrying continuity marker C-29 may open 29 days before term end without being late.
4. C-29 affects timing only; it does not relax discount approval bands from the enterprise approvals policy.
5. Renewals that include a new partner resale clause must also satisfy partner approval policy before signature.
